Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. Vibe Track works fully without an account. An account is only needed for things that involve other people or the cloud, such as friends and backup.

Who can read my notes?

Your notes are never sent to analytics. If you add friends, they see the entries you choose to share, plus your level and whether you're a member.

Is Vibe Track a medical app?

No. Vibe Track is not a medical service and does not diagnose anything. What it shows are patterns in your own entries. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services.

What does Premium cost, and how do I cancel?

$4.99 a month or $29.99 a year (prices may vary by region). You can cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings; you keep everything you've logged.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. CSV export of all your entries is free. You can also delete your account from Settings.
